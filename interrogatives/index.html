All three forms are correct French and all three are accepted. The inversion takes a euphonic -t- only when the verb form ends in a vowel — aime-t-il, a-t-elle, va-t-on, but est-il, prend-elle. Est-ce que elides before a vowel: est-ce qu'il. Inversion with je survives only in fixed forms (suis-je, ai-je, puis-je, dois-je, sais-je); the literary aimé-je is accepted but not taught. Because intonation is marked in writing by the question mark alone, that form needs its ? — the other two are marked by word order.
